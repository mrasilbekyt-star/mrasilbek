# Steady Doc — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

Steady Doc ("the game") is a single-player game. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## Data we collect

**None.** The game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

- The game does not ask you to create an account.
- The game does not connect to the internet.
- The game contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (stars per patient) and your settings (language, sound, vibration)
on your own device only. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing
its data in Android settings, deletes it.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads), this policy will be updated
before that version is released.
